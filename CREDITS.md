# 🌟 Credits, Sources & Acknowledgements

The **Bengali Data Hub (বাংলা ডেটা হাব)** is built upon the collective contributions of open-source maintainers, university researchers, and community data scientists. We extend our deepest gratitude to the creators and curators whose efforts make open research in Bengali AI, computational linguistics, and machine learning possible.

---

## 📌 Primary Open-Source Catalogs & Curated Resources

### 1. [BanglaKit / Awesome-Bangla](https://github.com/banglakit/awesome-bangla)
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/banglakit/awesome-bangla](https://github.com/banglakit/awesome-bangla)
- **Maintainers**: Aniruddha Adhikary and the BanglaKit open-source community
- **Description**: A comprehensive collection of tools, datasets, speech corpora, OCR benchmarks, and resources on Bangla computing and NLP.
- **Contributions to the Hub**: Seed listings for foundational corpora including OpenSLR ASR/TTS, Mendeley handwriting datasets (BanglaLekha, BanglaWriting, BN-HTRd), Mozilla Common Voice, and LDC language packs.

### 2. [BanglaNLP / bnlp-resources](https://github.com/banglanlp/bnlp-resources)
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/banglanlp/bnlp-resources](https://github.com/banglanlp/bnlp-resources)
- **Authors**: Firoj Alam, Md. Arid Hasan, Tanvirul Alam, Akib Khan, Jannatul Tajrin, Naira Khan, and Shammur Absar Chowdhury
- **Affiliations**: Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI), Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), BRAC University, University of Dhaka
- **Reference Paper**:
  > Firoj Alam, Md. Arid Hasan, Tanvirul Alam, Akib Khan, Jannatul Tajrin, Naira Khan, Shammur Absar Chowdhury.  
  > *"A Review of Bangla Natural Language Processing Tasks and the Utility of Transformer Models"*.  
  > arXiv preprint [arXiv:2107.03844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03844), 2021.
- **Contributions to the Hub**: 9 curated, standardized benchmark task datasets and train/dev/test splits (POS, Lemmatization, NER, Punctuation Restoration, Machine Translation, Sentiment, Emotion, Authorship Attribution, News Categorization).

### 3. [Sabbir Hossain Ujjal / Awesome_Bangla_Datasets](https://github.com/sabbirhossainujjal/Awesome_Bangla_Datasets)
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/sabbirhossainujjal/Awesome_Bangla_Datasets](https://github.com/sabbirhossainujjal/Awesome_Bangla_Datasets)
- **Maintainer**: Sabbir Hossain Ujjal
- **Description**: Curated directory indexing modern Bangla deep learning datasets across NLP, ASR, TTS, and OCR.
- **Contributions to the Hub**: Potrika newspaper dataset, Samanantar Indic parallel corpus, IndicCorp V2, Bangla Medical, and Bangla Poetry datasets.

### 4. [Elite Research Lab / BanglaNLP-Hub](https://github.com/eliteresearchlab/BanglaNLP-Hub)
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/eliteresearchlab/BanglaNLP-Hub](https://github.com/eliteresearchlab/BanglaNLP-Hub)
- **Maintainers**: Kishor Morol and the Elite Research Lab team
- **Description**: Community-maintained catalog of 67+ datasets, 800+ papers, models, and tools across 26 Bangla NLP tasks (DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22798635](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22798635)).

## 🏛️ Platforms & Community Hosts

- **[Mendeley Data / Elsevier](https://data.mendeley.com)**: Persistent open research data repository providing DOIs and peer-reviewed university data sets.
- **[Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co)**: Open-access machine learning community hosting model evaluation suites, tokenizers, and NLP benchmarks.
- **[Zenodo Open-Access Repository](https://zenodo.org)**: CERN / OpenAIRE open-science platform providing persistent Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs). Special thanks to academic authors from **BUET, DU, SUST, CU, DIU, BUBT, PUC**, and international research labs.
- **[Kaggle Community](https://www.kaggle.com)**: Open dataset community, competition hosts, and independent researchers across Bangladesh, including contributors like **Abir Kolin** ([Daraz Bangladesh Products & Reviews](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abirkolin/daraz-bangladesh-products-reviews)).
